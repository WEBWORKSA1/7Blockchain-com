Frequently asked questions

Do I need tax software?

If you made more than a handful of transactions, yes — manual cost-basis tracking across exchanges is where most filing errors come from.

Which cost-basis method should I use?

Depends on your country; many allow FIFO and specific-ID. The tools above let you compare methods before you file — pick the one allowed in your jurisdiction that minimises tax.
