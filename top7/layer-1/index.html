Frequently asked questions

What is a Layer-1 vs Layer-2?

A Layer-1 settles its own transactions and secures itself. A Layer-2 processes transactions off-chain and posts proofs/data to a Layer-1 for security — see our Top 7 Layer-2s.

Is ranking a blockchain even possible?

Only against stated criteria. Ours are published on /how-we-rank/; disagree with the weights and the order changes — that's the point of showing the metrics.
