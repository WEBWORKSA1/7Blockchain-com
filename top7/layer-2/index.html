Frequently asked questions

Are L2s safe?

Safer than most alt-L1s because they post data to Ethereum, but each has upgrade keys and sequencer trust assumptions. Check its stage on L2BEAT before parking large sums.

How do I move funds to an L2?

Use the official bridge or withdraw directly from an exchange that supports the L2 (Coinbase → Base, Binance → Arbitrum). Our Step 6 chapter walks through it.
