Frequently asked questions

Hardware or software wallet?

Both. Software for daily use and DeFi; hardware for anything you'd regret losing. Pair them: MetaMask or Rabby can sign with a Ledger.

What is a seed phrase?

12–24 words that regenerate your keys. Write it on paper or steel, never in a photo or cloud note. Anyone with the phrase controls the coins.

Are free wallets safe?

The software is; the risk is you — phishing, fake sites, blind signing. Our Step 4 chapter covers the seven rules.
