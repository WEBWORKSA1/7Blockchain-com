Frequently asked questions

Is spending crypto a taxable event?

In most countries, yes — converting crypto to pay is a disposal. Credit-mode cards (Nexo) avoid selling. Track it with a tool from our Top 7 Tax Tools.

Why do rewards keep changing?

Card rewards are marketing budgets; issuers tune them to market cycles. Treat the headline rate as a promo, not a promise.
