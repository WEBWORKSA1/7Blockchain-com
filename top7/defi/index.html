Frequently asked questions

Is DeFi safe?

Protocols above have multi-year track records and audits, but smart-contract, oracle and governance risks never reach zero. Size positions accordingly and never approve unlimited allowances from a wallet holding your savings.

How do I start?

Finish Step 6 of our learning path, use Rabby or MetaMask with a hardware wallet, start on Arbitrum or Base with $50, and only use protocols on this list.
